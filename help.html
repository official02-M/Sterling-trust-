FAQ
How do I reset my transaction PIN?

Go to Settings → Change PIN. You'll need your current PIN to set a new one.

Are transfers really instant?

Transfers between Sterling accounts are instant. External bank transfers are typically reviewed and completed within 1 business day.

How does savings APY work?

When you lock funds, we calculate simple interest at the displayed APY over the term. Your payout is credited at maturity.

Can I cancel a lock plan?

Lock plans are fixed-term and can't be broken early. They automatically release to your savings account at maturity.

What if I forget my password?

Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page, or contact support and we'll help you recover access.
